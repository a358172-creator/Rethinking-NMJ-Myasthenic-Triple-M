## Baseline and scope

The working tree was clean at inspection. The latter, frozen aggregate module, research sources and scientific validation reports are outside the editing scope. No deployment or source-data amendment is authorized by this illustration task.

The inspection covered `App`, every mounted anatomical SVG and its consumers, mechanism/evidence copy, Phase 1/2 scientific reviews, the Phase 3B integration audit, shared tokens, responsive styles, keyboard handlers, browser regressions and production exclusion checks. ## Design, scaling and accessibility

The Phase 3B design uses `tokens.css`: navy `#14283F`, slate `#243B53`, teal `#338A8A`, burgundy `#93485A`, muted amber `#BD8A42`, white and `#F7F9FA`. Anatomical depth will come from layered contours, cut surfaces and restrained shading within that palette. It will not restore the previously removed dark/neon figure styling.

Existing SVG groups already implement Enter/Space and `aria-pressed`; instance IDs use React `useId`. Global CSS respects reduced motion. Detailed illustrations currently shrink inside half-width columns, making embedded small labels difficult to read; the new detailed tissue view will have room for meaningful anatomy, external structure explanations and responsive controls. Small overview drawings will omit fine text instead of scaling it into illegibility. Scrollable detail views, where needed, will retain a keyboard-accessible named region. No hover-only information or continuous decorative particles will be introduced.

## Scientific geometry and evidence boundaries

- Presynaptic Ca-channel/release-site organization is informed by [Nishimune et al., 2004](https://www.nature.com/articles/nature03112); receptor-rich fold crests by [Fertuck and Salpeter, 1974](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC388231/); basal-lamina AChE localization by [Bernard et al., 2011](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20883790/). These experimental anatomical studies inform a conceptual mammalian endplate, not measured human ICI ultrastructure.
- Peripheral muscle nuclei, myofibrils and interstitial connective tissue/capillaries are checked against academic specimen guides from [Histology Guide](https://histologyguide.org/slideview/MH-055a-skeletal-muscle/04-slide-1.html) and [Duke](https://histology.oit.duke.edu/DPT/BasicTissues2/MuscleNerve.html). [Plomp et al.](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11782875/) and [Ucem et al.](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12988698/) support selected tissue observations; their findings do not establish the drawn pathology in every working-cohort record.
- Chamber/valve/outflow relationships and the conduction overlay are checked against [NHLBI anatomy](https://www.nhlbi.nih.gov/health/heart/anatomy) and [heartbeat physiology](https://www.nhlbi.nih.gov/health/heart/heart-beats). [Johnson et al., 2016](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa1609214) and the existing Giovannini/SITC references support the explanatory cardiac context, without importing new measurements.

Every path will be original editable vector work; no published illustration or histological micrograph will be copied. Drawings will identify orientation, compartments and schematic scale limits. Normal, hypothetical transmission impairment and conceptual muscle injury must remain different states. An intact synapse in the muscle example expresses a possibility, not universal normal NMJ function. Inflammation is a tissue-level concept, never proof supplied by myopathic EMG, antibody positivity, an ECG or the N=38 analysis itself. A conduction overlay cannot localize an inflammatory lesion from an arrhythmia. Preserved LVEF cannot exclude myocarditis.

## Implementation and validation plan

Create reusable SVG layers under `features/anatomy/components`, typed state/label data and scoped anatomy styles. Keep layer rendering separate from controlled state, structure selection and explanatory controls. Overview, comparison and laboratory adapters will reuse those layers, with stable per-instance definitions. Preserve the existing evidence panels, reference links, navigation, aggregate findings and release gates.

Capture the current overview, NMJ detail, comparison and laboratory anatomical view at 1440, 768 and 390 px before replacing geometry. Compare against the new normal and injury states at the same sizes. Record actual screenshot locations, anatomical additions, source limits and executed checks in `PHASE4A_VISUAL_VALIDATION.md`. Required checks include TypeScript, lint, build/publication exclusion, existing browser and aggregate regressions, new anatomical state/keyboard checks, accessibility and GitHub Pages base-path behavior. Software success does not constitute scientific-author approval or authorize deployment.

# Core atlas provenance review — Phase 3A.2

The targeted Siu review requires author resolution and blocks `CORE_ATLAS_VALIDATION=PASS`. Reproducing frozen categorical totals establishes numerical agreement with the workbook; it does not establish independent patients, correct source attribution or source-verified clinical categories. `CORE_ATLAS_PUBLICATION_APPROVAL` remains `PENDING` independently of scientific validation.

The [original Siu publisher abstract and disclosures](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/abs/10.2217/imt-2021-0225) describe seven presented patients, clinical care by the authors and consent obtained for the presented series. This evidence does not support the extraction's asserted split between one original case and six reviewed cases. Complete original case tables and supplements were not accessible during this review. The [PubMed record](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35321560/) establishes bibliographic identity, rather than an individual-case crosswalk.

The targeted comparison also found an extraction narrative matching a [separate original Gómez report](https://casereports.bmj.com/content/18/1/e260349) already represented in the frozen workbook. Its [original author-uploaded article](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/388257880_Triple_M_overlap_syndrome_following_pembrolizumab_treatment_importance_of_multidisciplinary_approach) is readable and corroborates this concrete source-attribution concern. Whether the records represent distinct patients remains unresolved. No patient equivalence was inferred from clinical similarity.

The linked original report exposes disagreements relevant to documented vital status and questions about case-specific antibody testing. It also supplies formal NMJ and conventional EMG evidence that must be assigned only after the correct original-case mapping is established. The individual values, categorical combinations, workbook identifiers, cell narratives and DOI crosswalk are retained exclusively in the new ignored, owner-only review files.

Author review must answer:

1. Which original Siu case and exact page/table support the selected extraction?
2. Which patients were reported originally and which were drawn from literature, with source locations for each classification?
3. Do the linked extractions represent distinct patients, and what primary evidence establishes their distinctness or duplication?
4. What explicit source statements support the affected antibody/testing categories, formal NMJ evidence, conventional EMG category and documented vital status, using a consistent follow-up endpoint?
5. What correction, if any, is approved after that review, and which independently justified validation requirements must be rerun?

The existing verification labels cannot answer those questions. Neither unique workbook labels nor the expected publication count demonstrate clinical distinctness. The unresolved mapping and category disagreements are required failed source-integrity checks, rather than warnings that permit release.

The frozen workbook, manuscript, membership, source assignments, expected targets and all earlier adjudication records remain unchanged. No corrected cohort, patient export or source reassignment was produced. Any amendment needs evidence and explicit author approval; release additionally requires a reviewed exact export and approval of any publication-policy change. Resolving this review would not validate the unresolved troponin analysis or authorize a private preview or public deployment.
